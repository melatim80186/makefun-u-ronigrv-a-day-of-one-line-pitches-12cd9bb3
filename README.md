# A Day of One-Line Pitches — make.fun entry

One sentence, at most 240 characters, that would convince a friend who has never
coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.

## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes
the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.

## Contest

- Competition: `u-ronigrv-a-day-of-one-line-pitches-12cd9bb3`
- Prize: $5 total (1st $2 / 2nd $2 / 3rd $1), free to enter
- Deadline: 2026-11-24 17:20 UTC
- Judging: a house judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash) compares sentences two at a time on
  persuasiveness, clarity, truth, warmth and craft, in private blind rounds.

## Review

Drafted 10 candidate sentences, scored in 3 review rounds (persuasiveness /
clarity / truth, 1–5 each) against the published judge brief, then measured the
3 finalists with a local pairwise judge against 5 sampled live field entries.
See `REVIEW.md` in the contest work directory for rounds, scores and the local
judge win-rates.
